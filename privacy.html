Transparency & Data Rights

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Our Core Principle: Zero Data Brokering

The Arc is designed to help you build personal discipline and athletic capability. We do not sell your personal data, rent user lists, or monetize your activity through behavioral advertising brokers. We collect only what is strictly necessary to run the service.

1. Information Collected During Pre-Registration

When you pre-register on this website to receive launch availability notifications, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used exclusively to deliver your pre-registration confirmation email and subsequent launch notifications.
  • Submission Timestamp: The exact date and time your request was recorded.
  • Network Verification & Security Data: A hashed identifier of your client IP address and user-agent string, retained temporarily to prevent automated spam abuse and enforce rate limits.

We do not collect names, payment card details, phone numbers, or physical mailing addresses on this pre-registration landing page.

2. Information Collected Inside The Arc Application

When you use The Arc progressive web application, data handling depends on your session type:

  • Offline Guest Mode: If you choose "Continue as Guest", 100% of your task completions, streak calculations, custom routines, and points are stored strictly on your local device in browser localStorage. No personal data or habit history is transmitted to our cloud backend in Guest Mode.
  • Authenticated Cloud Accounts: When you register an account, your profile data (display name, email address, password hash via Firebase Authentication), completed daily tasks, streak counts, all-time XP, and optional avatar image are synchronized to Google Cloud Firestore database so you can access your progress across devices.
  • Leaderboard Display: If you participate in the community leaderboard, only your display name and verified daily/all-time points are visible to other community members. Your email address is never made public.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Delivering pre-registration confirmations and launch availability notifications.
  • Authenticating your account and synchronizing daily habit records.
  • Calculating streaks, completion heatmaps, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Protecting platform infrastructure from malicious attacks, botting, and spam.
  • Responding to user-submitted help inquiries and bug reports.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted cloud infrastructure providers to operate The Arc. These providers are bound by strict data processing and confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud Firestore database, user authentication, and application hosting infrastructure.
  • Transactional Email Provider (e.g. Resend / MailerLite): Secure delivery of pre-registration confirmation messages and product release emails.
  • Web Hosting (GitHub Pages / Netlify): Static asset delivery and serverless API execution.

We never permit these infrastructure providers to use your information for their own independent marketing or to sell it to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Pre-registration email addresses are retained until The Arc reaches general availability or until you request removal. In-app account data is retained for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your profile, progress history, and leaderboard records are purged from our database.

6. Your Rights & Permanent Erasure

Regardless of your geographic location, you possess full control over your data:

  • Unsubscribe: Every launch notification email includes a direct, one-click unsubscribe mechanism. You may also email us directly to request immediate removal.
  • Right to Deletion (Right to be Forgotten): In the application, authenticated users can permanently delete all account records, calendar history, and leaderboard entries via Settings > "Delete my data".
  • Right to Access: You can request a summary of the personal information we hold associated with your email address.

7. Security Practices and Technical Safeguards

We implement industry-standard technical measures to safeguard your personal data:

  • All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Firestore databases are protected with role-based security rules and least-privilege access policies.
  • API keys and administrative secrets are isolated in server-side environment variables and are never exposed in client source code.

Please note: While we implement rigorous protections, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% infallible. We encourage users to maintain unique passwords and practice good digital hygiene.

8. Children's Privacy

The Arc is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or 16 and older in jurisdictions where local privacy regulations require higher age thresholds). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under these age limits. If we become aware that personal data from a child has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product updates, infrastructure modifications, or legal standards. Changes will be posted to this URL with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
