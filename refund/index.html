CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 · ARCRISE Protocol Operations

1. Overview & Pricing Tiers

At ARCRISE, our goal is to help you master discipline, workouts, and daily progression. All core habit tracking, daily schedules, workouts, and standard tools are permanently free.

For athletes seeking advanced analytics, 90-day macrocycles, and category radar insights, we offer optional ARCRISE Pro membership plans:

  • ARCRISE Pro — Monthly: ₹79 / month — Billed monthly. Auto-renews until cancelled.
  • ARCRISE Pro — Annual: ₹599 / year — Billed annually (~37% savings). Auto-renews until cancelled.
  • ARCRISE Sovereign — Lifetime: ₹1,499 one-time — Permanent offline & cloud access. Never recurs.

2. Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your recurring subscription (Monthly or Annual) at any time with immediate effect:

  • In-App: Navigate to Athlete Profile > The Arc Pro > Manage Membership & Billing and select Cancel Subscription.
  • Email Support: Send a cancellation request from your registered email address to arcrise.hq@gmail.com.

Upon cancellation, no further renewal payments will be charged. You will retain full access to all Pro features until the end of your current paid billing period.

3. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee (Refund Policy)

We offer an unconditional 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee on all initial membership purchases:

  • If you are not satisfied with your experience for any reason, request a refund within 7 days of your initial transaction.
  • To initiate a refund, email arcrise.hq@gmail.com with your registered email address and payment reference ID.
  • No questions asked. Once approved, your refund will be processed and returned to your original payment method (UPI, Bank Account, or Card) within 5 to 7 business days as per banking norms.

4. Duplicate or Failed Transactions

If your account was debited for a transaction that failed to activate your Pro status, or if an accidental duplicate charge occurred, our automated reconciliation will detect it or you can notify us immediately at arcrise.hq@gmail.com. Duplicate charges are refunded in full within 24–48 hours.
